# Training Creator Guide

Users with the **Creator (`creator`)** or **Administrator (`admin`)** role can author step-by-step guides, upload images and videos, publish to the catalog, and collaborate with team members.

---

## 1. Creating a New Training

1. Open the **My Trainings** tab and select **Create Training**.
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Training Title** (required)
   - **Description** using the rich-text editor (headings, bold, italic, lists)
   - **Cover Image** (optional — `JPG`, `PNG`, `WEBP`, `GIF`, `AVIF`)
   - **Categories**
   - **Visibility:** Enable **Available to all users** if you want the training to appear in the main catalog once published.
3. Click **Save Draft & Configure Steps** to open the editor.

---

## 2. Building Steps & Uploading Media

Inside the editor, switch between **Training Details** and **Steps**:

1. In the **Steps** tab, enter a **Step Title** and write detailed instructions in the rich-text editor.
2. Under **Step Images & Videos**, attach one or more photos or videos (`MP4`, `WEBM`, `MOV`, `MKV`).
3. Use the up/down arrows to reorder attachments within a step or reorder steps across the training.
4. Click **Add This Step** (or **Save Step Changes** when editing an existing step).

---

## 3. Publishing or Archiving

1. Switch to the **Training Details** tab in the editor.
2. Choose the **Publication Status**:
   - **Draft:** Visible only to authorized creators and editors.
   - **Published:** Live in the catalog for learners.
   - **Archived:** Hidden from the active catalog. If you open an archived training for editing later, Smart Guide will ask if you want to unarchive it back to Draft.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

---

## 4. Sharing Access with Collaborators

1. Open the training and click **Share**.
2. Search for a user by name or email and select their access level:
   - **Owner (`owner`):** Can edit, share, and delete the training.
   - **Editor (`editor`):** Can edit details and steps, but cannot share or delete.
   - **Viewer (`viewer`):** Read-only study access (ideal for private trainings that are not visible to the entire company).
3. Click **Grant Access**. The original creator is protected from accidental removal.
