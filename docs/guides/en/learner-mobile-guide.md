# Learner & Mobile App Guide

This guide explains how to connect the Smart Guide mobile app to your organization's server, study trainings, download content for offline use, and export PDF guides.

---

## 1. Connecting the Mobile App to Your Server

When opening the mobile app for the first time, you will see the **Connect to Server** screen:

- **Using a QR Code (fastest):** Tap **Scan QR Code** and point your camera at the QR Code shown under **Admin > Instance Settings** (provided by your administrator).
- **Manually:** Type your server URL (for example, `https://training.example.com`) and tap **Connect**.

Once connected, sign in with your email and password or with **Google** / **Microsoft** (if enabled by your organization).

---

## 2. Browsing & Studying Trainings

1. In the **Trainings** tab, use the search bar or category filters to find a guide.
2. Opening a training displays the **Overview** screen with its description, step list, and progress bar.
3. Tap **Start Study** (or **Continue Study**) to enter step-by-step mode:
   - Read the instructions and tap any image or video to **expand it fullscreen**.
   - Tap **Mark step as completed** as you finish each step to save your progress.
   - Use **Previous Step** and **Next Step** to navigate.

> **Note:** Opening a training from **My Trainings** (author preview mode) does not record learner progress until opened from the main **Trainings** catalog.

---

## 3. Offline Mode

1. On the training overview screen, tap **Download Offline**.
2. Wait for the steps, images, and videos to finish downloading. Once complete, the **Saved Offline** badge appears.
3. You can now open and study the training without an internet connection. When you are back online, the app automatically checks for updates published by the author.
4. To free up storage space on your device, tap the trash icon next to **Saved Offline**.

---

## 4. Exporting to PDF

On any training page, select **Export PDF** at the top to generate a formatted document with a cover page, table of contents, step text, and attached images for printing or archiving.

---

## 5. Changing Language

You can switch the interface language between **Português (`pt-BR`)**, **English (`en`)**, and **Español (`es`)** at any time using the language selector in the header or on the login screen.
